Why payments to India stall

Most delays are decided before the money moves.

A cross-border payment rarely goes wrong on the day it's sent. It goes wrong on a detail nobody checked the week before.

  1. 01
    Returned for missing or inconsistent information.
    A name, account or purpose that doesn't line up, and the payment comes back.
  2. 02
    Documents requested after submission.
    The invoice or contract gets asked for once the payment is already moving.
  3. 03
    Beneficiary mismatch.
    The details you hold don't match your supplier's bank records.
  4. 04
    Compliance back-and-forth.
    Questions arrive one at a time, and every round adds time.
  5. 05
    A missed cut-off.
    One late answer, and execution slips.

Straiton does the checking first, so it isn't happening while your supplier waits.
